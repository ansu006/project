My Dance Journey ♡

When I was younger, I didn't know how to dance. Everything changed when a dance teacher visited my school, taught us a routine, and explained why people choose to dance.

That experience made me curious. I started discovering the joy of dancing, and slowly, I fell in love with it.

Dance has become an important part of my life. It makes life feel more entertaining and gives me a way to express myself. The curiosity that started with dance also encourages me to learn and explore other things.
